Say "Orson", wait for the tone, then say one line. Wait for the reply before the next line.

## Part 3: corrections, then check

1. No, the meeting with Arjun is at twelve, not eleven.
2. When is my meeting with Arjun? (12)
3. Actually, I got the drill back from Mr. Shah.
4. Who has my drill? (you have it back)

## Part 4: things that shouldn't be stored

1. What is the capital of Australia? (Canberra; nothing stored)
2. Tell me a joke. (a joke; nothing stored)
3. My car is up folks who are going to tie gun. (asks you to repeat; nothing stored)
4. I wonder where I left my keys. (searches memories, doesn't store)

## Device commands (handled on the phone, no Ormem call)

Stop. Louder. Quieter. Repeat that. Mute. Unmute.
